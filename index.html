Digital Awareness & Safe Application Guide

Equip yourself with the knowledge to apply online safely and protect yourself from fraudulent scams.

1

Verify the Genuine Portal Domain

Always confirm the web address ends in .gov.in or .nic.in. Never submit personal identity details on `.com`, `.org`, or third-party blog pages.

2

Review Official Eligibility Criteria

Read age thresholds, annual family income caps, landholding records, and residency rules carefully on the scheme guidelines document before applying.

3

Prepare Digital Copies of Required Documents

Keep Aadhaar linked to your active mobile number (for OTP eKYC), bank account linked to NPCI for DBT (Direct Benefit Transfer), and updated income certificates.

4

Complete the Application Yourself or at CSC

Fill out the application directly or visit an authorized Common Service Centre (CSC) or Gram Panchayat office. Authorized CSCs charge only fixed nominal government service fees.

5

Save Application Reference Number & Track Online

Download the acknowledgment slip or take a screenshot of your registration/acknowledgment ID. Use this ID to track approval status on the official portal.

Never Pay for Free Schemes

Central and State government welfare registrations (PM-KISAN, Ayushman Bharat, PMAY) are strictly 100% free. Anyone demanding commissions or upfront bribes for 'guaranteed approval' is committing a crime.

Never Share OTP or Banking PINs

Government officials, bank managers, or call centers will never call asking for your Aadhaar OTP, UPI PIN, or bank passwords. OTP is only required when you initiate an eKYC transaction yourself.

Beware of WhatsApp & Telegram APKs

Never install `.apk` files sent over WhatsApp claiming to be "Govt Yojana App" or "Instant PM Money Transfer". These are spyware trojans designed to empty your bank balance.

Report Cyber Fraud Immediately to 1930

If you or someone in your family has accidentally transferred money to a scammer or shared banking credentials, dial the National Cyber Crime Helpline 1930 or report on cybercrime.gov.in within the golden hour.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to common questions about applying for and receiving government benefits.

Yes. The Government of India and State Governments do not charge any application or registration fee on official portals like PM-KISAN, Ayushman Bharat, or NSP. If you use a private Cyber Café or CSC (Common Service Centre), they may only charge a small standard government-approved fee (typically ₹20–₹50) for scanning and printing services.

DBT stands for Direct Benefit Transfer. Money is transferred straight into your Aadhaar-seeded bank account via NPCI (National Payments Corporation of India). To ensure you receive DBT, visit your bank branch and submit the "Aadhaar Seeding and NPCI Mapping Consent Form" or check your status on the official UIDAI portal.

Official Indian government portals always end with .gov.in or .nic.in (e.g., https://pmkisan.gov.in). Fake websites usually end in .com, .in, .org, or .xyz and often have sensational headlines like "Get ₹10,000 in your account immediately click here". Never enter your bank or personal data on non-gov websites.
